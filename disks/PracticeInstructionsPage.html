Please read the following instructions carefully.

At every trial, you will see six decorated discs.
5 out of 6 are exactly the same; only one disc is different; this is the "oddball".
You have to find the oddball as quick as possible and click on it.

You can take a break in between trials, but once you click on the cross icon to start the next trial, you have to respond as fast as possible. Both speed and precision matter.
You will be given feedback after each trial.
We will start with a practice session consisting of three trials.

When you are ready, click the button to start the practice session.
